(`types/`, `library/schema/`, `library/dataset/openapi.yaml`) are MIT so that shipping a client is never a
licensing problem. The single file `library/dataset/core_facts.json` is CC0 and stays that way forever.

2. **You may not** do (1) in a product or service that carries revenue — sales, subscription, ads, paid

3. **You may not** strip provenance, rename the dataset as your own compilation, or publish derived
   commentary that presents an editorial reconstruction as a source statement. Rows we mark
   `no_source_ruling`, `cite_only`, `suspected_mislabel` or `polarity_reliability: low` must keep that
   marking in anything you redistribute.
4. **No warranty, and no claim of efficacy.** The material documents what named authorities wrote. It is
   not medical, legal or financial advice, does not assert that any technique predicts anything, and must
   not be marketed as doing so. Any product built on it should carry the equivalent of

5. **Third-party rights are not granted here.** Text we ship comes only from works whose registry
   disposition is `full` (public domain or openly deposited). Where a row is `cite_only`, the text is
   deliberately absent and you get a pointer, nothing more. Ifá verses and any living-lineage material are
   excluded by policy, not oversight — see `LICENSE_POLICY.md`.
6. **Versioning.** A licence attaches to a release tag. `main` is unlicensed draft material: pin a tag
